## 2026-06-22 (Monday)

### Did

- **Step 13 (carried from W25)** — `reconciler.py`: `reconcile()` diffs
  target weights against the current book and returns `[sells] + [buys]`,
  all sells preceding all buys (ADR 0005 invariant); shares sized as
  `floor(equity × weight / price)`; 7 unit tests plus 4 cross-sectional
  engine integration tests, including a rotation regression proving buys
  fail if executed before sells
- Added `CrossSectionalMomentum`: native `CrossSectionalStrategy` scoring
  each ticker by trailing total return from T-skip-lookback to T-skip
  (12-1 month convention); skip avoids short-term reversal
- Fixed a cash-availability bug in cross-sectional buys: the reconciler
  sizes target shares from stale T-1 mark-to-market equity and T-close
  prices, but execution happens at T+1 open with slippage, which can
  exceed available cash — added a cash-availability check before each buy,
  taking the minimum of a proportional-commission bound and a
  minimum-commission-floor bound; a buy that still can't afford one share
  is silently dropped (Phase 5 was flagged to add underfill tracking and
  proportional allocation across pending buys)
- Wrote per-strategy writeups (`journal/strategies/`) for all seven Phase 4
  strategies plus a comparison table
- Wrote `phase_4_retro.md`; closed Phase 4 in `CLAUDE.md` (bumped current
  phase to 5) and `PROJECT_PLAN.md`
- Completed and committed the W25 weekly journal retroactively

### Learned

- **No single-asset Phase 4 strategy beats 0050 buy-and-hold consistently.**
  `CrossSectionalMomentum` (Sharpe 0.779) is the only strategy that clears
  the benchmark on a risk-adjusted basis — with the caveat that its CAGR is
  overstated by survivorship bias in the 2026 universe snapshot. Reported
  as a finding, not hidden, per the project's honesty-over-impressiveness
  principle.
- **MACD is cost-dominated.** ~92% turnover on TSMC burns ~NT$30.8k in fees
  per ticker versus ~NT$7.6k for SMA, negating any gross edge — a reminder
  that turnover economics can erase a strategy's apparent alpha before
  Sharpe is even computed.
- **The cross-sectional cash-availability bug is a symptom of a real
  problem class, not a one-off.** Sizing from stale prior-bar equity while
  executing at a slipped T+1 price is exactly the kind of gap Phase 5's
  partial-fill and rejection semantics exist to handle properly, rather
  than working around with an inline cash check.
