### Learned

- **`BarBatch` was a deliberate deviation from the `Iterator[Bar]` sketch in
  `PHASE_5.md` §4.9.** Cross-sectional strategies and `NO_MARKET_DATA`
  detection both need a full day's tickers together, not a flat per-ticker
  stream — the spec's own sketch turned out to be the wrong shape once step
  8 was actually built, and the fix was to batch by date rather than force
  the panel-shaped consumers to reassemble a flat stream themselves.
- **`share_multiplier()` unifies two call sites that used to duplicate
  ratio-convention logic.** `LotLedger.on_split()` and `rejection.py`'s
  ex-day reference-price formula both need "new shares per old share" for
  the same action types; extracting one function instead of keeping two
  independent branches removes a place the two could silently drift apart.

## Retrospective

### What went well

- **Steps 7 and 8 landed together in one session with no rework.** Both
  build directly on the event-sourced spine from W27; the corporate-action
  decomposition's purity (no direct account mutation) meant it composed
  cleanly with the bar feed work without either needing to wait on the
  other.

### What was harder than expected

- **The `BarBatch` deviation from the spec's own sketch needed to be
  caught and justified, not just implemented.** `PHASE_5.md` §4.9 sketched
  `Iterator[Bar]`; building step 8 against that literally would have left
  cross-sectional consumers reassembling per-ticker streams into panels
  themselves. Catching the mismatch during implementation, rather than
  building to the letter of the spec, was the right call but took real
  judgment.
